National Toll Plaza Traffic & Corridor Intelligence — India (SFC India)
National road-network traffic, toll-plaza and corridor intelligence
An internal tool for accessing and analysing toll-plaza traffic volumes across the national road network. It maps annual vehicle throughput at fee plazas by category (Car/Jeep, LCV, Bus/Truck, 3-Axle, 4–6 Axle, OSV), and overlays strategic freight nodes including cement/mining clusters, steel plants, industrial hubs, ports and ICDs to support origin–destination and network-level traffic assessment. Filter by vehicle metric, state, traffic intensity, and freight-node category; export the current view or the full dataset.

Current data: FY 2025–2026  |  Source: IHMCL website — ETC Transaction Reports – IHMCL
Note: This dataset does not distinguish between buses and trucks; both are reported under a single combined “Bus/Truck” category. Users must keep this limitation in mind when interpreting commercial-vehicle figures. The tool is intended for preliminary assessment only. Developers: Satish Kedar Nath and Sumit Oraon.

Traffic intensity classification: For the selected vehicle category and current geographic/minimum-traffic filters, toll plazas are ranked by annual traffic volume and divided into quartiles: Low = bottom 25%, Medium = 25th–50th percentile, High = 50th–75th percentile, and Very High = top 25%. Thresholds therefore recalculate dynamically when the vehicle type or underlying filters change. Bubble size also scales with traffic volume; darker blue shades and stronger outlines indicate higher intensity.

Source & interpretation

Country: user-supplied India boundary GeoJSON. States: Esri India Living Atlas 2024 State Boundary layer. Corridors: nearby toll-plaza pairs are first selected by distance, then the displayed corridor is routed along the actual road network using OSRM. Line thickness: traffic strength at both endpoints. Coordinate correction: Nangli Toll Plaza (Plaza Code 239600) is explicitly corrected to Karnataka at 13.194547, 78.545287 based on official NETC/NHAI records; the source workbook contained an erroneous 25.648871, 92.047617 coordinate.
Coordinate update: 367 toll-plaza coordinates from the uploaded toll_plaza_coordinates (1).xlsx file have been incorporated into the GIS database. These records retain their original traffic counts; no random coordinates were assigned.
Freight nodes: 156 strategic freight nodes from India_Freight_Nodes.xlsx are incorporated as a separate reference layer. Coordinates are indicative WGS84 centroids from the supplied workbook and should be verified before survey-grade distance analysis.
